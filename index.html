Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

1. Who we are

The Automation Layer ("we", "us") operates an automated publishing service that uploads and manages videos on YouTube channels whose owners have authorised it. The service is operated from the United Kingdom. This policy explains what data we access, why, how long we keep it and how you can remove it.

2. Use of YouTube API Services

This service uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Data we access

When you authorise the service with your Google account, we request only the permissions needed to run it:

  • Upload and manage videos: to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publish status, and to mark altered or synthetic content.
  • Read channel information: channel and video statistics (for example subscriber, view and upload counts) and channel standing, used to pace uploads safely.
  • Read YouTube Analytics: aggregate performance metrics for videos the service published, used to choose future topics.

We never see or store your Google password. We do not access your contacts, email, viewing history or any data unrelated to the authorised channel.

4. How we use the data

  • To upload and publish videos you have approved.
  • To check channel health before uploading, so we do not upload while a channel has a problem.
  • To measure which videos perform well and plan future content for the same channel.

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI models.

5. Storage and security

OAuth access and refresh tokens are held in an encrypted credential store on our server and are used only by the publishing service. Access to the server is restricted to the operator. Tokens are refreshed automatically and are never shared with third parties.

6. Retention and deletion

  • OAuth tokens are kept until you revoke access or ask us to delete them, and are deleted within 7 days of that request.
  • Channel statistics and analytics we store are refreshed regularly and kept for no longer than 90 days, unless kept in aggregate form that does not identify a channel.
  • You can ask us to delete all stored data relating to your channel at any time using the contact below.

7. Revoking access

You can revoke the service's access at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections page (also reachable from Google security settings). Revoking access stops all uploads immediately.

8. Sharing

We do not share Google user data with third parties, except where required by law or to protect against fraud or abuse. Videos and their metadata are, by design, published to YouTube at your direction.

9. Cookies

This website does not set cookies or use tracking or analytics scripts.

10. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes affecting how Google user data is used will be notified before they take effect.

12. Contact

For questions, data requests or token deletion: support@yourdomain.com
