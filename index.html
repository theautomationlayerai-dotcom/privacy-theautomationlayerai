Privacy Policy for TheAutomationLayerAI Upload Service

Last updated: 9 October 2026

1. Overview

We operate an automated video upload service that can upload videos to your YouTube channel on your behalf when you authorize our application. Our service uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. This policy explains what data we access, how we use it, and how you can revoke access.

2. Data We Access

When you authorize our application we request permission to upload videos to your YouTube channel (scope: youtube.upload). We do not access or store your Google password. We store OAuth tokens (access and refresh tokens) securely to perform scheduled uploads.

3. How Tokens Are Stored and Used

OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest and stored only for the purpose of uploading videos and refreshing access. Tokens are rotated and refreshed automatically. We do not share tokens with third parties.

4. Use of Google User Data

TheAutomationLayerAI's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google user data only to upload and schedule the videos you ask us to. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models, and no person reads it except where you ask us to for support, where it is needed for security, or where the law requires it.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep OAuth tokens only while your authorization is active. When you revoke access or ask us to delete your data, we delete stored tokens and any data obtained through YouTube API Services within 30 days. We do not use cookies or similar tracking technologies on this site.

6. Revoking Access

You can revoke our access at any time from your Google Account third-party connections page (Google Account → Security → Third‑party apps with account access). You can also request token revocation by contacting support at theautomationlayerai@gmail.com.

7. Content and Copyright

You are responsible for ensuring you have the rights to any voice, likeness, or media you ask us to upload. If you request voice cloning or human likenesses, you must provide written consent from the person(s) involved.

8. Contact

For questions or to request deletion of stored tokens, email: theautomationlayerai@gmail.com
